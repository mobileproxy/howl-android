Howl is the VPN client for vpn.how. Add your subscription once — the app does the rest.

WHY IT KEEPS WORKING

Howl does not rely on a single way in. Your subscription carries several independent protocols to the same servers, so a block on one of them does not cut you off:

• VLESS with Reality
• Hysteria2
• Shadowsocks-2022
• ShadowTLS
• OpenConnect
• AmneziaWG

The app measures every available server by real latency and connects through the fastest one. A built-in watchdog notices when a server stops responding or the network changes — Wi-Fi to mobile, roaming, a dead hotspot — and reconnects on its own, without you opening the app.

WHAT YOU CAN CONTROL

• Pick a country and a protocol yourself, or leave it on automatic
• Split tunnelling: send chosen sites straight past the VPN
• Per-app routing: decide which apps use the tunnel
• Works with Android's always-on VPN and "block connections without VPN"
• Russia mode: banks, government services and marketplaces that reject foreign addresses keep working

WHAT IT DOES NOT DO

No account inside the app, no registration, no advertising, no analytics profile. Your subscription link is all it needs.

A vpn.how subscription is required to use this app.
